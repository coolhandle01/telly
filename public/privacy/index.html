Privacy policy

telly · last updated 18 September 2026

telly builds a day’s television schedule out of the YouTube channels you subscribe to, and plays the programmes in YouTube’s own player. This policy covers the version of telly published at telly.na-n.xyz. It explains what telly reads from your YouTube account, where that information goes, how long it is kept, and how to remove it.

Who is responsible for telly

telly is made and published by one person as a hobby project. There is no company behind it, no telly account to create, and no server of telly’s own. telly runs inside your browser, and the person who publishes it receives none of the information described in this policy.

The permission you give

When you sign in, Google asks you to grant telly one permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

It is read-only. It allows telly to read the channels you subscribe to and the videos those channels have uploaded. It does not allow telly to post, comment, subscribe, unsubscribe or delete anything, and it does not give telly your name, your email address or your watch history.

What telly reads from your YouTube account

  • An identifier YouTube uses for your account.
  • The channels you subscribe to, and their names.
  • For each of those channels: the topics YouTube files it under, and its subscriber count where the channel publishes one.
  • For each of those channels, its twenty most recent uploads: the title, how long it runs, when it was published, the category and tags it was uploaded with, its view count, whether YouTube has age-restricted it, whether it is declared as made for children, whether it can be played outside YouTube, and whether it is live.

telly uses this to decide which of its five stations a channel belongs to, and what time of day its programmes go out. It reads nothing else from your account.

Where that information goes

It goes from YouTube to your browser, and stays there. telly has no server to send it to and no database to put it in. The person who publishes telly cannot see it. No copy of it is sent to any other company, and none of it is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

telly shows you no advertising, loads no analytics or tracking software, and sets no cookie of its own.

What is kept on your device, and for how long

  • The schedule. What telly reads from your account is saved in your browser’s storage on this device, so that switching the set on again does not have to fetch it all from YouTube a second time. It is kept for up to 24 hours. After that telly stops using it, and replaces it the next time you watch.
  • Your sign-in. Google gives telly a pass that lets it read your subscriptions for about an hour. telly holds that pass in the page only, and closing the tab ends it. It is not saved anywhere on your device. When the hour runs out, telly asks Google for another one without a further sign-in screen. If Google declines, telly returns to its signed-out state.
  • A note that you signed in here. telly records in your browser that a Google permission screen was completed on this device, so that returning to the page does not have to ask you again. It is a yes or no, and says nothing about you. It is removed when you sign out, and when Google declines to let telly continue.

How to remove it

  • Sign out in the page. This hands your permission back to Google, and removes the saved schedule belonging to your account. If someone else has signed in to telly on this device, their saved schedule is left alone. If your browser refuses to remove the record, or telly cannot tell which record is yours, the page tells you so.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser. This removes everything telly has put on your device, including schedules saved by other accounts.

Taking the permission back at Google

You can withdraw telly’s permission from your Google account at any time, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. telly can then no longer read your subscriptions, and the page returns to its signed-out state. Doing this does not remove the schedule already saved on your device. Signing out in the page does that, and so does clearing this site’s data.

When you are not signed in

telly plays a small set of sample programmes built into the page. It reads nothing from any YouTube account, and asks YouTube for none of your data. The page does load Google’s sign-in software when it opens, so Google receives a request from your browser at that point, as it would on any page offering a Google sign-in button.

Other companies involved

  • Google and YouTube. Signing in, and reading your subscriptions, happen directly between your browser and Google. Google’s privacy policy covers what Google does with those requests.
  • YouTube’s player. Programmes play in YouTube’s own embedded player. That player belongs to YouTube, and it may set cookies and record what you watch, as it does on YouTube itself. telly cannot see or control this. The YouTube Terms of Service apply to that part of using telly.
  • GitHub. telly’s pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives each request for the page, and its documentation states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and kept for security purposes. What GitHub does with that is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your subscriptions do not pass through GitHub. They go between your browser and Google.

The connection

The pages telly serves, and the requests it makes to Google, use HTTPS. A plain http:// address for this site is redirected to the HTTPS one, which means such a request leaves your browser before it is redirected.

Google API Services User Data Policy

telly’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

telly is not directed at children, and requires a Google account to sign in to. telly asks for no profile information, and reads no name, email address or date of birth.
