Your Google account

Signing in grants telly read-only access to your subscriptions and their recent uploads. You may withdraw that at any time from Google’s security settings, and telly then has no access to your subscriptions and shows no programmes.

The sign-out control in the page does the same withdrawal and also clears the schedule data this browser has saved. The privacy policy describes both halves.
